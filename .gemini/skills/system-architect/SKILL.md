# System Architect & ADR Generator

## Architecture Blueprint
1. **Context & Goals**: High-level problem statement, business requirements, non-goals.
2. **Key Trade-offs**: Latency vs. Throughput, Consistency vs. Availability, Simplicity vs. Flexibility.
3. **Component Diagram**: Mermaid diagram of services, databases, queues, and client boundaries.
4. **Data Model & Schema**: Entities, relationships, indexing, partition keys.
5. **Failure Modes & Resilience**: Circuit breakers, retry policies, graceful degradation.
